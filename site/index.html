Local-first personal agent

Your week, read back to you every Monday.

Pulse connects activity across Gmail, GitHub, and Notion into a weekly review of decisions, commitments and stalled work. Open the evidence behind each claim. Claude interpretation is optional.

The loop

Five quiet steps, once a week.

01

Ingest

The last 7 days from every connected source.

02

Store

Normalised events written to a local SQLite file.

03

Analyse

Evidence and momentum computed locally. Claude interpretation is optional.

04

Brief

A Markdown briefing drafted for the week ahead.

05

Deliver

Optionally emailed to you when you pass --send.

Private by construction
No server. No cloud. Nothing leaves that doesn't have to.
  • ✕ Email bodies — only the subject and a 120-character snippet are ever kept.
  • ✕ Notion page contents — only titles, status, and edit times.
  • ✕ Credentials or tokens — gitignored, never written to the database.
