01 / Connect

One view of the week

Gmail, GitHub and Notion activity resolve into workstreams in a local SQLite ledger. Missing source coverage stays visible.

02 / Verify

Read the evidence

Decisions, commitments and blockers link to captured records. External claims and unverified source content keep their labels.

03 / Decide

Leave with an action list

Review an item, choose follow-up or waiting, write your next step and download a plan. Reviewing a promise never marks it fulfilled.

A personal review, on your terms

Your work history
stays in your ledger.

Pulse runs locally. The public workspace uses only invented records; it never connects to your accounts.

The default review is deterministic. If you configure optional model interpretation, selected titles and short snippets are sent to that model provider. Credentials are kept outside the ledger.
